HOMELAB

Welcome to my lab. Here I'm open to sharing what all I run and host in my homelab. I'm quite proud of it.

  • Some of my favorites include the following:
  • Wazuh An open-source security information and event management (SIEM) solution.
  • Nessus Scanner: A powerhouse of a vulnerability scanner.
  • HomeAssistant on a Raspberry Pi 4: cus I like to tinker with automations, and it's nice to tune my lights.
  • Proxmox Hosts: Virtualization platform for running VMs on a single host.
    • One host is a Ryzen 9 5950X with 64GB of RAM, and the other is an Intel i7 with 24GB of RAM.
    • These run way too many services to list here. If you want to learn more, Contact Me is a great place to start.
  • UGREEN DH4300+ NAS: A 4-bay NAS with a Ryzen 5 4500U and 8GB of RAM. Great little box for hosting my media, content, docs, and backups.
